Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how Ecommerce ("we") handles information when you use this application.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name and email address. If you register with a password, we store a password hash rather than your plain-text password.
  • Sign-in details from Google or Atlassian when you choose those options, such as your provider account ID, name, and email address, subject to the information that provider makes available.
  • App activity needed to provide the store, such as cart contents and, for administrators, product details and uploaded product images.
  • An authentication cookie used to keep you signed in. Logging out clears this cookie but does not by itself delete your account data.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, operate the store and shopping cart, display products, and provide support.

Services that process information

Google and Atlassian process information when you choose their sign-in options. Account and store data is stored in the database configured for this application. Product images uploaded by administrators are processed and hosted by Cloudinary. These services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and store information while it is needed to operate the application. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information, contact us through the support link below. We may retain information where required for legitimate operational or legal needs.

Security

We use safeguards such as hashed passwords and protected authentication cookies. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the application changes. For questions or privacy requests, contact the project maintainer through the project support page . Please do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive personal information in public support requests.
